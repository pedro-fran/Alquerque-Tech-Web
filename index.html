Instructions

  • Each player has pieces of their own color; the pieces sit on the line intersections. You play with the blue pieces (at the bottom).
  • Movement: move a piece to an empty neighboring position along a line, without going backwards. Diagonals only exist at some positions.
  • Capture: jump over a neighboring opponent piece to the empty position right behind it, in any direction. The captured piece leaves the board.
  • Multiple captures: after capturing, if you can keep going, you must keep going. You cannot pass through the same position twice.
  • If you have no possible move, use “Pass turn”.
  • End of the game: the winner is whoever still has pieces when the opponent has none left.

How to play in this application

  1. The pieces that can move are highlighted. Click one.
  2. Click one of the highlighted destinations. In a multiple capture, click each jump.
  3. Click the piece again to cancel the selection.
